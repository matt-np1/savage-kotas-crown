1. How to use this

  1. Read the game plan. It is the master plan, the strat we race from.
  2. Open the tool to make your own copy and try ideas. Your edits are saved on this device only.
  3. Reset in the tool returns your copy to the master plan at any time.
  4. Send your version to Matt from the tool, with a short note on what you changed and why.

2. The game plan

Our plan is built on using every rider as hard as we can. Every watt and every kilojoule gets spent. We use every rider up at the right time so we cross the line with six riders plus a backup.

Everyone knows their own effort, and everyone knows where they are meant to be burnt up. Designated sectors show where you give effort and where you save it.

We're all rolling most of the time, just taking key "business class" breaks when needed, and longer single-line turns when the watts are spare.

If you last longer than you're supposed to you haven't given enough. If we finish with 8 riders we failed.

Charles Milner medal is for Bishops boys who grew up on participation trophies
